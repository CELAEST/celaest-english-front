# 01. PRODUCT VISION: Lingua Platform Scope & Experience

> **Target Version**: 1.0.0  
> **Core Pillars**: Adaptive AI Mentor, Cognitive Calm, Professional Growth  

---

## 1. Product Summary

**Lingua** is an intelligent language mastery platform designed for software engineers, product managers, founders, and global professionals who need high-precision English fluency. 

Unlike traditional language learning platforms that test memorization through passive drills, Lingua acts as an active **AI Language Partner & Cognitive Coach**. It evaluates speaking speed, phonetic hesitation, sentence structure complexity, and contextual vocabulary usage during real conversations, reading sessions, and writing challenges.

---

## 2. Core Feature Modules

### 2.1 Onboarding & "Learning DNA" Assessment
- **Interactive AI Onboarding**: The user engages in a 4-step conversation with the glowing AI Orb.
- **DNA Profile Construction**: The system dynamically identifies career goals (e.g., Programming, AI, Executive Leadership), speaking confidence level, preferred practice length, and natural learning style.
- **Summary Blueprint**: Generates an initial personalized strategy dashboard ("Learning DNA Ready").

### 2.2 Live Conversation Hub (`Workspace` & `Conversation`)
- **Central Orb Interface**: A 3D interactive glowing orb (`#7048E8` glow) that reacts dynamically to voice input (`Listening`, `Analyzing`, `Speaking`).
- **Real-Time Audio Waveform**: High-precision visual audio bars indicating speech volume and pitch dynamics.
- **Contextual Suggestions**: Soft pill recommendations ("Your weekend", "A challenge you faced", "Something you learned") to eliminate blank-page anxiety.

### 2.3 Interactive Memory Bank ("Turn Mistakes into Mastery")
- **Flip-Card Correction Mechanism**: Dual-sided cards revealing "You said: *He don't like coffee*" vs "Better way: *He doesn't like coffee*".
- **Grammar & Syntax Explainers**: Interactive "Why is this better?" drawer with AI Mentor tips.
- **Spaced Repetition Categories**: Cards organized by state (`To Review`, `Learning`, `Mastered`).

### 2.4 Knowledge Graph & Adaptive Roadmap
- **Visual Skill Topology**: Interactive graph representing grammar nodes, business vocabulary clusters, and fluency milestones.
- **Journey Timeline**: Day-by-day progress stream showing past sessions, today's focus, and upcoming target scenarios.

### 2.5 Active Reading Studio
- **Dual-Pane Focused Reader**: Curated high-level business and tech articles.
- **Instant Definition & Pronunciation**: Click any word to open an ambient dark popover displaying IPA pronunciation (`/'materz/`), contextual translation, and single-click "Add to Memory".
- **Focus Mode**: Fullscreen distraction-free reading with ambient audio support and live progress ring.

### 2.6 Professional Writing Lab
- **Structured Challenges**: Real-world scenarios (e.g., "Write an email to a client explaining a project delay").
- **Real-Time AI Proofreader**: Highlights severe word choices, tone misalignments, and length issues as the user types.
- **Reflection & Feedback Summary**: Post-writing analysis dissecting structure, vocabulary range, and conciseness.

### 2.7 Simulated Mock Interviews
- **Timed Pressure Sessions**: Realistic job interview practice (e.g., Product Manager, Senior Engineer).
- **Live Speaking Timer & Pace Tracking**: Radial timer keeping track of speaking speed and hesitation pauses.

## 3. Product Experience Principles

| Principle | Description | Reference Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cognitive Space** | Deep black backgrounds (`#04040A`) with wide padding (40px+) to focus attention strictly on the active task. | Apple / Linear |
| **Ambient AI State** | The AI is always present as a glowing visual entity (Orb/Waveform), never hidden in a secondary chat widget. | OpenAI / Raycast |
| **Instant Micro-Feedback** | Subtle color highlights (Mint `#10B981` for mastery, Lavender `#B89CFF` for suggestions) instead of disruptive popups. | Stripe / Cursor |
| **Keyboard Fluidity** | `ESC` to exit focus, `Cmd+K` / `Ctrl+K` for command palette, `Space` to speak/pause. | Raycast / Warp |
