# Reading Practice Mobile Adaptation Standard (CELAEST)

## Architectural Principle: Mobile Ergonomics & Strict Desktop Invariance
- **Strict Desktop Invariance**: Desktop and tablet breakpoints (`sm:`, `md:`, `lg:`, `xl:`) must never be altered or degraded when adapting mobile layouts.
- **Top Navigation Removal on Mobile**: In mobile (`< sm:`), the top-left `< Workspace` button is hidden with `hidden sm:inline-flex` because the bottom floating dock (`WorkspaceSidebar`) already provides direct navigation without cluttering the reading viewport.
- **Reading Cosmic Orb Proportion**: Set to `w-28 h-28` (112px) on mobile, establishing a linear geometric progression across all breakpoints (112px mobile -> 128px sm -> 144px md -> 160px lg -> 176px xl). This fills the vertical canvas elegantly while maintaining plenty of clearance above the reading article and bottom navigation dock.
- **Justified Text & Typographic Spaces (Editorial Alignment)**: To ensure the right edge aligns straight and flush just like the left edge, the reading container uses `text-justify [text-align-last:left] [text-justify:inter-word] [hyphens:auto] w-full`. Instead of artificial fixed margins that block justification, regular words emit real typographic space strings (`" "`) between spans, allowing the browser's line layout engine to justify lines cleanly without ragged edges.
- **Phrasal Verb Cohesion**: Multi-word phrasal verbs (e.g. "step in", "rule out") preserve tight `mr-[1.5px] sm:mr-[2px]` and omit the space string internally, ensuring connected phrases stay unified as a single badge and never break or stretch across lines.
- **Clip Prevention & Fluid Scrolling**: The `article` container has `overflow-y-auto no-scrollbar` so lengthy passages never get cut off at the bottom of compact phone screens.
- **Vocabulary Modal Popover Clamping**: Modal `left` coordinate is clamped to `Math.max(22, Math.min(left, window.innerWidth - popoverWidth - 14))` to account for the `-left-[18px]` floating speaker audio icon.
- **Completion View & Zero Bottom Bar Collision**: When `isCompleted` is true, `ReadingBottomBar` is NOT rendered (`!isGenerating && !isLoading && !isCompleted`). `ReadingCompleteView` provides its own complete actions ("Next reading", "Re-read article") and telemetry. Showing `ReadingBottomBar` simultaneously caused a double-bar collision where `< Previous 03/03 Next >` drew directly on top of `SESSION TELEMETRY`.
- **SVG Dimension Integrity (Anti-Explosion)**: Custom SVG components (like `ComprehensionQuizIcon` and `ReadingSuccessIcon`) must always use valid, standard Tailwind classes (e.g. `w-4 h-4 sm:w-5 sm:h-5` and `w-11 h-11 sm:w-14 sm:h-14`) rather than non-existent fractions (`w-4.5`, `w-13`), preventing the SVG from losing width/height and ballooning into a screen-dominating circle.
- **Bottom Dock Clearance on Completion View**: `ReadingCompleteView` applies `pb-20 sm:pb-6` and `overflow-y-auto no-scrollbar` to guarantee full readability and comfort above the 68px floating mobile dock on any mobile device.
