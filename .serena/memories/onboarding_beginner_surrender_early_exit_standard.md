# Onboarding Diagnostic: Beginner Surrender Detection & Early-Exit Protocol

### Pedagogical & Behavioral Standard
- When a candidate explicitly expresses that they do not speak English ("no se ingles", "no hablo inglés", "no entiendo", "i don't speak english", "cero ingles"):
  - **Do NOT ask further questions**: Forcing an absolute beginner through subsequent questions or demanding they describe complex workplace tasks or projects causes severe user frustration and embarrassment.
  - **Immediate Compassionate Conclusion**: The interview terminates immediately. The AI responds with genuine warmth and empathy in Spanish, validating their honesty and reassuring them that Lingua is built specifically to take learners from zero to fluency.
  - **Instant A1 Calibration**: Calibrate level directly to `A1 — Beginner`, `compositeScore: 20`, `speakingConfidence: "Low"`, `conversationStyle: "Foundational & Step-by-Step"`.
  - **Fast Forward to Roadmap**: The chat input is replaced with the `View Learning Roadmap ->` button, enabling the learner to begin their foundational lessons immediately without friction.
