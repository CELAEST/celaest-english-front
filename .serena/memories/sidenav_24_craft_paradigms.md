# Sidenav Design Matrix: 24 Precision Craft Paradigms (CELAEST Standard)

## Context
Directly in Design Lab (`/lab`), the Sidenav Evolution Matrix was expanded from 6 to 24 radically unique, production-grade architectural paradigms without discarding any previous work.

## Anti-AI Gimmick & Pure Precision Standard
- Eradicate tacky AI cliches: no neon rainbow glows, no cyber particles, no pulsating holograms, no fake AI sparkles.
- Adopted physical luxury and architectural discipline:
  1. Editorial Suizo (Vignelli crosshair, Jony Ive 0.75px hairlines, Kyoto origami, 0px stealth blade, Haute Horlogerie guilloche bezel, Peter Zumthor Therme Vals basalt).
  2. Hardware & Mecánica (Dieter Rams Braun ET66/Vitsoe 606, Leica M11 optical rangefinder, Teenage Engineering TP-7 analog reel, Studio Cockpit VU meter, Acoustic Monolith).
  3. Flotantes & Docks (macOS kinetic dock, Cosmos pebbles, Horizon bottom action deck, Liquid mercury tension, Dynamic Island top pill, 28px Kyoto Sensu silk ribbon).
  4. Estructuras & Bloques (Linear dual-rail 48px, Bento 3-cardlet island, Tadao Ando 45-deg titanium, Notion tree hierarchy, Chronological fluency laser spine).

## Live Activation
All 24 paradigms map dynamically to live sidebar variants (`atelier_minimalist`, `precision_chrono`, `specular_glass`, `acoustic_resonance`) via CustomEvent `celaest:sidenav_variant_changed` and `localStorage.getItem("celaest_sidenav_variant")`.
